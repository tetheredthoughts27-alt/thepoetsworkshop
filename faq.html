Before You Register

Questions.

Who can register?

Adults, 18 and older.

When do sessions meet?

Six Sundays, January 10 – February 21, 2027, from 5 to 7 PM ET on Zoom.

What if I miss a session?

Every participant gets one private mentor call with Bean, scheduled anytime within the workshop dates. If you know you'll miss a session, let Bean know in advance and your call can cover what you missed — you stay eligible to submit as a finalist. Emergencies are handled at Bean's discretion. If you don't miss anything, the call is still yours to use. Finalists receive additional mentorship leading up to the show date.

How large is the audience if I'm chosen?

Up to 200 people, live, in Dayton.

What's the rehearsal commitment for finalists?

Rough-draft rehearsals begin May/June 2027; promo recording happens during this stretch. More frequent rehearsals through July and August. Performance in September–October 2027.

Who owns my piece?

You do. Copyright stays with you. If you're chosen, you agree not to perform, publish, submit to journals or contests, or share the piece publicly from selection through the last performance. The day after the run closes, all restrictions lift.

How are finalists chosen?

You submit your final piece one week after the last session. Judges score against the rubric. Finalists notified by email within seventeen days.

Scholarships?

Two community fee waivers. First-come, first-served. Send us a message and tell us why this workshop matters to you.

Refund policy?

Full refund before Session 1. None after.

Content advisory.

This workshop lives in consequence, desire, and memory. What you write here is what you may read and submit for finalist selection. Come prepared to write the poems you've been avoiding.

Get in touch.

Received. We'll be in touch.
