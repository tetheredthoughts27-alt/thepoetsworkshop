A six-session writers workshop exploring the cyclical nature of healing — led by poet Leroy D. Bean. You'll write into two acts. The first is the leap. The second is what's underneath when the music stops.

Two finalists perform their piece at a live production in Dayton, 2027 — with Bean's mentorship carrying them from the page to the stage. Every participant also receives a private mentor call with Bean.
